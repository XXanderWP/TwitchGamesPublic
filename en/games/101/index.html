Liars Bar

Get rid of all your cards, bluff your friends, and hope for a blank shot — a thrilling and fun game where every move could be your last!

Game rules

  1. Goal: be the last player alive.
  2. At the start of each round, all alive players get cards and one rank becomes the table card.
  3. On your turn, play from 1 up to the card limit. Cards are placed face down.
  4. You may throw any cards, but only the table rank or Joker are safe from a lie call.
  5. The next player either throws cards or presses "Call liar" on the previous move.
  6. If the liar call is correct, the previous player is punished. If it is wrong, the caller is punished.
  7. Punishment is a Russian roulette shot: each punishment increases your chance to die.
  8. Turns are timed. If time runs out, the AFK penalty selected in lobby settings is applied.
